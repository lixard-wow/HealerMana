# HealerMana

**Know your healers' mana before the pull goes sideways.**

HealerMana shows you the mana of every healer in your group or raid, all in one small, clean display. No more tabbing through frames trying to figure out who's about to go oom — just glance at the grid and you know.

---

## What It Does

- Shows every healer's mana as icons or bars, updated live
- Choose a vertical list, horizontal row, or grid layout — whatever fits your screen
- Healers dim when they're out of range, so you know at a glance who's too far away
- Each healer is color-coded by class, with their spec icon shown too
- Sort your healers alphabetically or by class
- Choose which content it shows up in — open world, dungeons, raids, scenarios, battlegrounds, arenas — turn any of them on or off
- Resize it, move it, lock it in place once you like where it sits
- Everything you set up is remembered automatically — no need to redo it next time you log in

Works with every healer spec in the game: Holy/Discipline Priest, Restoration Druid, Holy Paladin, Restoration Shaman, Mistweaver Monk, and Preservation Evoker.

---

## How to Use It

Once installed, it just works — the display shows up automatically any time you're grouped with a healer (including yourself, if you play one).

Type `/hm` any time to open the settings window and change how it looks and behaves.

### Getting Set Up

1. Install it and log in — nothing else to do.
2. Group up with at least one healer.
3. Type `/hm` to open settings — pick icons or bars, adjust size, spacing, and sorting until it looks right.
4. Drag the display wherever you want it on your screen.
5. Lock the frame from the settings window so you don't accidentally move it.

### Commands

| Type this | To do this |
|---|---|
| `/hm` | Open the settings window |
| `/hm raid <number>` | Show fake test healers so you can preview a layout solo (`/hm raid off` turns it off) |
